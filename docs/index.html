K1X

Kick drum. 909, 808, and friends.

You wanted a kick you can actually tune. K1X is a one-shot with 909, 808, 707, Tight, and Hard styles: body, click, decay, then drive, filters, extra layers, Mix, a parallel FX send, and post EQ. Length and reverb predelay can snap to the DAW tempo, so the tail lands on the grid instead of wherever it felt like going.

It is part of Stupid Audio — five free instruments in one pack. The other four live over there.

VST3 and standalone. Windows, Linux, and Mac.

Main

This is the kick: waveform, Style, Shape, Hold, and the knobs that make a thud into a drum. Play and Loop live here in standalone. In a DAW you hide those and hit it with MIDI instead.

  • Style — 909, 808, 707, Tight, Hard. Changes click, envelopes, and a bit of harmonic. Does not reset your knobs.
  • Shape — sine (default) or triangle body.
  • Hold — one-shot dies after the decay; Hold keeps the tail going until the next note.

Layers

The rail next to the wave adds Bass, Noise, Click, Sweep, or Sample on top of the kick. Each slot can sit a little early or late (0–80 ms), and each one has its own EQ. The strip under the wave is always that 80 ms window, so you can see who is late to the party.

Mix

Duck and Time dip the kick body (and Bass) when the beater hits, so the click can punch through. Then Kick and slot faders, with mute, solo, phase, and Send into the FX. Optional stem outputs if your host actually uses extra pins.

Effects

A parallel send after the layered mix: Dist (Soft, Hard, Fold, Diode), delay, and reverb. Dry stays the unprocessed mix. Sync snaps delay time and reverb predelay to the tempo. Spectrum is what actually leaves the plugin.

EQ

Five-band parametric on the selected layer, and the same editor as post EQ on Effects. Drag a node, wheel for Q, double-click to reset a band. The spectrum sits behind the curve so you can see what you are doing to the thud.

Download K1X

The Windows installer is the easy path. It can put the VST3, the standalone app, or both on the machine, and it adds a Start menu shortcut for the app.

Windows installer

The VST3 zip is just the plugin. Drop it where your DAW looks for VST3s. Mac builds are ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper will sulk until you right-click Open the first time.

Windows Linux macOS

The standalone zip is the app, no DAW required. Same Gatekeeper story on Mac. Linux wants the usual system ALSA, FreeType, fontconfig, and X11 libraries.
