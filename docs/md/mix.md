# Mix

Duck and the faders live here. Main / Effects in the title bar (next to the patch name) switches pages; Mix is the blending desk for the kick and the extra slots.

- **Duck / Time** — a 0.5–4 ms dip on the kick body and Bass slots when the click/beater hits. 0 is off. Click, Noise, Sweep, and Sample stay put, which is the point.
- **Kick and slot faders** — mute, solo, phase, and **Send** (how much of that layer goes into the FX send, default full).

## Outputs

Stereo **Output** is always the full mix (Width, send FX, post EQ), so a host that ignores extra pins still hears the whole drum.

Optional extra stereo buses **Kick**, **Slot 1**, **Slot 2**, **Slot 3** are that mix layer after mute/solo/gain, before Width and post FX (L=R). Leave them off if you do not need stems.
